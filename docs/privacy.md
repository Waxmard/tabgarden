# tabgarden privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-30

## What tabgarden reads

tabgarden reads the titles and URLs of the tabs in your current window, in memory only, to decide how to group them. Grouping runs on your device, either with Chrome's built-in AI (Gemini Nano) or by each tab's site hostname.

The first time you group tabs or use Smart clear, Chrome asks you to allow tabgarden to "Read your browsing history". Chrome uses that wording for any extension that can see tab titles and URLs. tabgarden never reads your history list; it only sees the tabs open in your current window. Closing tabs after the active one and ungrouping work without this permission.

## What tabgarden collects, stores, or shares

For Smart clear, tabgarden stores how many seconds per day you spend focused on each site, recorded by hostname only (for example `mail.google.com`), never full URLs or titles. Days older than 60 days are deleted. This data stays in local extension storage on your device; it is never synced or transmitted. Removing the extension deletes it.

tabgarden sends nothing over the network. It uses no cookies, analytics, or third-party services. Your data is never shared or sold.

## Changes to this policy

If these practices change, this page will be updated and the date above revised.
